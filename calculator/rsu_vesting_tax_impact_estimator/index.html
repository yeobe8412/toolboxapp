RSU Vesting Tax Impact Estimator

Estimate the tax impact of an RSU vesting event by applying a user-supplied effective tax rate to the vest-date market value. The result shows gross compensation value, estimated tax, net value after the modeled tax, and the approximate number of shares needed to cover that tax if shares were withheld at the same price.

IRS materials generally treat RSU value delivered to an employee as wage compensation for U.S. federal reporting, but payroll withholding is not necessarily the same as final tax liability. This calculator is therefore a flexible scenario tool, not a jurisdiction-specific withholding table.

1. Enter the RSUs vesting
Use the units expected to settle in the vesting event you want to estimate.

2. Enter the vest-date price
Use a scenario market price per share for that date.

3. Set the effective tax rate
Combine the tax components you want represented into one rate.

4. Review the tax estimate
The main result is the modeled tax impact on the gross vest value.

5. Compare share equivalent
The approximate shares-for-tax figure shows how many shares the estimated tax would represent at the entered price.

Gross vest value = RSUs vesting × share price at vest
Estimated tax impact = gross vest value × effective tax rate
Estimated after-tax value = gross vest value - estimated tax impact
Approximate shares for tax = estimated tax impact ÷ share price at vest

Where:

  • gross vest value = market value of units at the entered price
  • effective tax rate = combined user-entered rate
  • shares for tax = economic equivalent of the tax amount at the same share price

Assumptions: Actual employer withholding can differ from final liability and may use different rounding or settlement procedures.

What the result means

Withholding and final tax liability can differ. Check current tax rules and your employer’s plan and payroll treatment.

Change one assumption at a time to compare scenarios and understand which input has the largest effect on the result.

Given:

  • 1,200 RSUs
  • Vest-date share price: $75
  • Effective tax rate: 37%

Calculation:
Gross vest value = 1,200 × $75 = $90,000. Estimated tax impact = $90,000 × 37% = $33,300. The after-tax value is $56,700, and $33,300 ÷ $75 is about 444 shares.

Result:
Estimated vesting tax impact = $33,300.

Interpretation: If shares were used purely as an equivalent measure at $75 each, the tax amount corresponds to about 444 shares.

Is the tax amount the same as what my employer will withhold?

Not necessarily. Withholding is a payment toward tax, while final liability depends on your complete tax situation. Use the rate that best matches the scenario you want to test.

Why does the calculator not use a fixed supplemental wage rate?

Rates and methods can depend on jurisdiction, payment method, employer payroll treatment, and current rules. A user-entered rate avoids presenting one withholding rule as universally applicable.

Are RSUs taxed before they vest?

This calculator does not determine legal tax timing. It is designed around a vesting-event value because employee RSU compensation is commonly reported when the award settles under the applicable plan and tax rules.

Can I include payroll and state taxes?

Yes, by adding them into the effective tax rate you enter. The tool does not calculate each tax separately.

How is this different from the RSU after-tax value estimator?

This page emphasizes the tax amount itself. The after-tax value estimator emphasizes the value left after tax and can also include separate deductions.
