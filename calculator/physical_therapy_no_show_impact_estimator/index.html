Physical Therapy No Show Impact Estimator

Estimate the weekly operational impact of physical therapy no-shows by translating missed appointments into lost treatment hours, realized visit volume, and potential revenue at a user-entered value per completed visit. The calculation starts with scheduled visits and the no-show rate, so it can be used for either a recent week or a planning scenario.The revenue figure is deliberately labeled as potential value rather than guaranteed collections. Actual financial impact depends on payer mix, rescheduling, cancellation fees, same-day backfilling, visit authorization, and whether staff time can be redeployed.

What the result means

The main result is the expected number of missed visits for the entered schedule and no-show rate. Secondary results show how those missed slots translate into time and simple visit value.

This is an operational estimate. It does not account for late cancellations, wait-list backfilling, rescheduled visits, collection rates, or payer-specific reimbursement.

Given

  • 225 scheduled visits/week
  • 6.5% no-show rate
  • 55 minutes/visit
  • $125 average value per completed visit

Calculation

No-shows = 225 × 0.065 = 14.625 visits. Completed visits = 210.375. Lost hours = 14.625 × 55 ÷ 60 = 13.41 hours. Potential value = 14.625 × $125 = $1,828.13.

Result

Estimated no-shows: 14.6 visits/week; lost time: 13.4 hours/week; potential visit value: $1,828.13.

The estimate quantifies the opportunity tied up in missed slots before considering any appointments that can be refilled or recovered later.

Is a cancellation the same as a no-show here?

Only if your organization reports it that way. Use a rate that matches the definition in your scheduling data so the estimate stays consistent.

What should I use for average visit value?

Use the metric relevant to your analysis, such as average allowed amount, average net revenue, or another internal visit value. Do not mix gross charges with collected revenue unless that is intentional.

Does a missed visit always create a full lost hour?

No. The calculator uses the entered average visit duration. Some staff time may still be used for outreach, documentation, or another patient if the slot can be filled.

How can I model same-day backfilling?

Reduce the effective no-show rate to the share of scheduled slots that ultimately remain unused, or run a second scenario using the net unfilled-slot rate.

How is this different from the patient capacity estimator?

Capacity estimates what the clinic could deliver under stated resources. This tool measures how scheduled demand is reduced when booked appointments are not completed.
