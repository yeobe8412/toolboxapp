TikTok Ads Email Calculator

This calculator estimates how efficiently a TikTok advertising campaign turns paid traffic into email subscribers. It connects ad spend, impressions, clicks, and completed email sign-ups so marketers can see both the size of the acquisition funnel and the cost of each new subscriber.

Use it when promoting a newsletter, lead magnet, waitlist, product launch, or gated resource through TikTok Ads. The result helps compare creative variations, landing pages, and audience segments on a consistent basis. A lower cost per email is usually preferable, but subscriber quality and downstream revenue should also be reviewed before shifting budget.

Count confirmed email submissions attributed to the campaign.

5. Review the funnel
Compare cost per subscriber with CPM, CTR, click cost, and page sign-up rate to locate the weak stage.

Cost per email subscriber = TikTok ad spend ÷ Email sign-ups
CPM = Ad spend ÷ Impressions × 1,000
CTR = Clicks ÷ Impressions × 100
Email sign-up rate = Email sign-ups ÷ Clicks × 100

Where:

  • TikTok ad spend: campaign cost in currency
  • Email sign-ups: completed subscriber acquisitions
  • Impressions: total ad displays
  • Clicks: landing-page clicks

Assumptions: All inputs must cover the same attribution window. The calculator does not adjust for duplicate, invalid, or later-unsubscribed addresses.

What the result means

Paid acquisition cost for each completed email sign-up.

Use the result with consistent cost, attribution, and counting definitions when comparing campaigns.

Given:

  • Ad spend: $720
  • Impressions: 144,000
  • Clicks: 2,160
  • Email sign-ups: 324

Calculation:
Cost per email = $720 ÷ 324 = $2.22
CPM = $720 ÷ 144,000 × 1,000 = $5.00
CTR = 2,160 ÷ 144,000 × 100 = 1.50%
Sign-up rate = 324 ÷ 2,160 × 100 = 15.00%

Result: $2.22 per email subscriber.

Interpretation: The campaign acquired 324 subscribers at a little over two dollars each; the supporting rates show that both ad engagement and landing-page conversion contributed to that outcome.

Should I use link clicks or all clicks?

Use clicks that can reasonably lead to the email form, ideally landing-page views or outbound link clicks. Including likes, profile taps, or other interactions will understate the sign-up rate.

What counts as an email sign-up?

Count completed submissions attributed to the campaign. For double opt-in lists, using confirmed subscribers gives a stricter and usually more useful acquisition cost.

Why can cost per email improve while CTR falls?

A better landing page or stronger offer can convert a smaller number of clicks into more subscribers. Review the full funnel rather than treating CTR as the only success measure.

How should zero sign-ups be interpreted?

The cost per email is undefined when no subscribers are acquired. The calculator shows a dash while still reporting upstream metrics that may help diagnose the issue.

Is cost per email the same as email CPA?

It is a specific CPA where the conversion action is an email subscription. A general email CPA calculator may use purchases, demos, downloads, or another defined action.
