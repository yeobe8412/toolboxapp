Home Time Estimator

The Home Time Estimator calculates the weekly and annual time spent on recurring household tasks. It helps households evaluate cleaning, cooking, laundry, maintenance, and administrative workload, and can clarify where time-saving changes may have the greatest effect.

The result is based on task duration and weekly frequency. It does not judge whether the time is efficient; instead, it converts a routine into a consistent total that can be compared with available personal time or divided among household members.

What the result means

The main result is the total active time the household spends on the entered tasks each week.

Overlapping tasks and passive waiting time should be counted only if you consider them active household work.

Given:
- Cleaning: 90 minutes × 2 sessions
- Cooking: 60 minutes × 6 days
- Other tasks: 180 minutes/week
- Two people share the work

Calculation:
Weekly minutes = 90 × 2 + 60 × 6 + 180 = 720 minutes
Weekly hours = 720 ÷ 60 = 12 hours
Per person = 12 ÷ 2 = 6 hours/week
Result: The routine requires about 12 household hours per week, or 6 hours per person if divided evenly.

Should travel time for errands be included?

Include it in Other task minutes if it is part of the household workload you want to measure.

How should multitasking be counted?

Avoid double counting two tasks performed at the same time; count the active block once unless they genuinely require separate attention.

Can the per-person figure reflect an unequal split?

Not directly. The displayed per-person figure assumes an even division of the total workload.

Why use 52 weeks for the annual estimate?

It provides a simple annualized view of a repeating weekly routine.

Does the tool estimate outsourced service cost?

No. It measures time only; a cost estimator would require hourly rates or service prices.
