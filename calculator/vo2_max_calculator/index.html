VO2 Max Calculator

The VO₂ Max Calculator estimates maximal oxygen uptake from a field test. Choose a 12-minute run estimate based on distance or a 1-mile walk estimate that also uses time, heart rate, age, weight, and sex as defined by the selected equation.

Field-test estimates are useful for tracking aerobic fitness trends, but they are not equivalent to a laboratory metabolic test. Health status, pacing, terrain, temperature, medication, and measurement error can affect the result.

What the result means

Which method should I choose?

Use the equation that matches the field test you actually completed.

Is a laboratory test more accurate?

Generally, a supervised metabolic test directly measures respiratory gases and is more precise.

Can medication affect the walking test?

Yes. Drugs that change heart rate can distort heart-rate-based estimates.

What units does the result use?

The result is milliliters of oxygen per kilogram of body mass per minute.

Is the fitness label a diagnosis?

No. It is a broad descriptive range and not medical advice.
