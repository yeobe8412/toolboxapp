Pig Feed Nutrient Rate Estimator

Estimate the mass of a selected nutrient delivered by a pig feed program. The calculation uses pig count, feed offered per pig per day, nutrient concentration, and the number of days to translate a formulation percentage into a total nutrient quantity for the group.

The tool is useful for feed budgeting and formulation comparisons when you already have an appropriate ration specification or feed analysis. It does not calculate the nutrient requirement of pigs at a given weight or production stage. Nutrient requirements and practical feeding decisions depend on factors such as growth stage, genetics, health, environment, feed intake, digestibility, and the specific nutrient being considered. Keep the feed amount and nutrient percentage on the same as-fed or dry-matter basis.

Feed per day = Number of pigs × Feed per pig per dayNutrient per day = Feed per day × (Nutrient concentration ÷ 100)Period nutrient = Nutrient per day × Days

Where:

  • Number of pigs = animals receiving the ration
  • Feed per pig per day = kg of feed per pig per day
  • Nutrient concentration = selected nutrient percentage of the feed
  • Days = planning period

Assumptions: This calculation measures nutrient supplied by the entered ration. It does not judge whether that amount meets the pigs’ requirement.

What the result means

The result is the total kilograms of the selected nutrient supplied by the entered pig-feed program over the chosen period.

For ration adequacy, compare supply with appropriate feeding standards or professional nutrition guidance for the specific pig group.

Given

  • 180 pigs
  • Feed: 2.7 kg/pig/day
  • Nutrient concentration: 17.2%
  • Period: 10 days

Calculation
Feed per day = 180 × 2.7 = 486 kg/day.
Nutrient per day = 486 × 0.172 = 83.592 kg/day.
Period nutrient = 83.592 × 10 = 835.92 kg.

Result
835.92 kg

Interpretation
The modeled ration supplies 835.92 kg of the selected nutrient to the group over ten days.

Can the nutrient percentage be entered on a dry-matter basis?

Yes, but the feed amount must also be expressed on a compatible dry-matter basis. Mixing as-fed feed amounts with dry-matter nutrient percentages will distort the result.

Does this calculator account for nutrient digestibility?

No. It multiplies feed mass by the stated nutrient percentage. If you need digestible or available nutrient supply, use a concentration value already expressed on that basis or use a more detailed nutrition model.

Should I enter feed offered or feed actually eaten?

Use actual intake when you want nutrient consumption. Use feed offered when the planning question is about what the feeding program delivers, understanding that refusals and waste may lower intake.

Why can the result be large over a long period?

The output accumulates the nutrient mass for every pig across every entered day. Check pig count, feed rate, percentage, and period if the total seems unexpected.

How is this different from feed conversion ratio?

Nutrient rate translates a feed concentration into nutrient mass supplied. Feed conversion ratio compares total feed consumed with live-weight gain and does not identify individual nutrients.
