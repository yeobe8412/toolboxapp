Digital Assessment Cost per Learner Calculator

The Digital Assessment Cost per Learner Calculator allocates assessment program costs across the learners served in the same measurement period. It brings platform fees together with item development, administration or proctoring, and support costs so teams can see a broader unit cost than license price alone.

The output is an average cost per assessed learner and includes a breakdown between platform and non-platform expense. It can support budget comparisons between delivery models or reporting periods, but only when cost scope and learner counts are defined consistently. A lower average unit cost may reflect scale rather than better assessment quality, so the result should be considered alongside completion, reliability, security, and operational performance metrics.

What the result means

The result is the average selected assessment-program cost allocated to each learner assessed during the period.

This average does not show marginal cost for one extra learner and does not measure assessment quality or validity.

Given:
$24,000 platform cost
$15,000 item development
$27,000 administration/proctoring
$6,000 support and other cost
1,800 assessed learners

Calculation:
Total cost = 24,000 + 15,000 + 27,000 + 6,000 = $72,000
Cost per learner = 72,000 ÷ 1,800 = $40.00
Platform portion = 24,000 ÷ 1,800 = $13.33 per learner

Result:
$40.00 per assessed learner

The selected program costs average $40 per assessed learner, with about one-third of that amount attributable to the platform.

Which learners should be in the denominator?

Use learners who were actually assessed during the same period as the costs. If you instead use assigned learners or licensed seats, label the metric differently because the denominator changes the interpretation.

Should retakes be counted as additional learners?

Not for a learner-based denominator unless you intentionally want cost per attempt. If retakes materially drive cost, consider calculating both cost per learner and cost per assessment attempt.

Can proctoring be a variable cost?

Yes. This calculator accepts the total proctoring or administration cost for the period, regardless of whether that cost was fixed, hourly, or per attempt.

Why might cost per learner fall as volume rises?

Fixed costs such as platform fees or initial item development are spread across more learners. Variable costs may still rise with volume, so the average does not reveal the exact marginal cost.

How is this different from LMS cost per learner?

This metric focuses on assessment delivery, item development, administration, and support. LMS cost per learner covers the broader learning-platform and course-delivery environment and may include different cost categories.
