Bathroom Remodel Material Requirements Estimator

The Bathroom Remodel Material Requirements Estimator calculates planning quantities for bathroom floor tile, wall tile, and backer board after applying a common waste allowance. It is intended for homeowners and remodelers who have measured the surfaces to be covered and want an early purchase quantity before checking package sizes and supplier requirements.

The estimator keeps the three surface types separate so you can see how the same waste assumption affects each one. It does not account for tile pattern, niche geometry, tub or shower openings, sheet layout, waterproofing overlap, trim pieces, or product-specific coverage. Confirm final dimensions and installation specifications before ordering materials.

Use the adjusted areas to calculate boxes, sheets, or boards based on product coverage.

Adjusted material area = Measured surface area × (1 + Waste allowance %)

The same percentage is applied independently to floor tile, wall tile, and backer board areas.

What the result means

The main result is the floor-tile purchase area after the selected waste allowance is added.

Round adjusted areas up to full cartons, boards, or sheets using the actual coverage printed for the product.

Given: Floor tile = 60 ft²; wall tile = 135 ft²; backer board = 110 ft²; waste allowance = 10%.

Calculation: Floor = 60 × 1.10 = 66 ft². Wall = 135 × 1.10 = 148.5 ft². Backer = 110 × 1.10 = 121 ft².

Result: Plan for 66 ft² of floor tile, 148.5 ft² of wall tile, and 121 ft² of backer board before package rounding.

Interpretation: The waste allowance adds 6 ft², 13.5 ft², and 11 ft² respectively to the measured quantities.

Should I subtract the vanity footprint from floor tile area?

Measure the surface you actually plan to tile. If tile will not continue beneath a fixed vanity, exclude that footprint from the measured floor area.

Do I subtract doors and windows from wall tile area?

Large openings can be deducted if they will not be tiled, but small deductions may be offset by cuts and waste. Use the net surface area that best matches your installation plan.

Is one waste rate suitable for every material?

Not always. This tool uses one rate for convenience. You may need separate calculations when tile and board have very different layout or breakage characteristics.

Does backer board area include overlap for waterproofing membrane?

No. Backer board and waterproofing products have different installation rules. Follow each manufacturer’s coverage and overlap requirements separately.

How is this different from a bathroom budget planner?

This estimator produces physical material quantities. A budget planner converts project categories into monetary totals and contingency.
