Family Split Calculator

Split a shared family expense among household members using either equal shares or income-based weights. The calculator shows each person’s share for a four-person setup and can accommodate fewer people by leaving unused weights at zero.

This approach helps families document a consistent contribution method for rent, utilities, travel, or caregiving expenses. It does not determine what is legally owed and should be used as an agreed budgeting method.

Formula:

Each person’s share = total expense × (person’s weight ÷ total of all active weights). For an equal split, enter the same positive weight for every participating person.

What the result means

Person 1 share is the central planning estimate produced from the values you enter.

A zero weight excludes that person from the split. Equal weights create equal shares.

Given: A $2,400 expense and income weights of $5,000 and $3,500 for two participating family members.

Calculation: Total weight = $8,500. Person 1 share = $2,400 × 5,000 ÷ 8,500 = $1,411.76. Person 2 share = $988.24.

Result: The two shares add back to $2,400 and reflect their relative income weights.

How do I make an equal split?

Enter the same positive weight for every participating person.

Can weights represent percentages?

Yes. They can be incomes, percentages, points, or any consistent positive scale.

What happens when a weight is zero?

That person receives a zero share and is excluded from the active weight total.

Will the shares always equal the total expense?

Yes, apart from display rounding. The underlying proportional shares sum to the entered expense.

Is an income-based split always fair?

Fairness depends on the family’s agreement and circumstances. The calculator only applies the selected weighting method.
