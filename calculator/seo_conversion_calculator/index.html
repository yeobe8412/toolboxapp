SEO Conversion Calculator

The SEO Conversion Calculator measures or forecasts conversions generated by organic search traffic. It works for purchases, registrations, downloads, demo requests, or any other clearly defined conversion event.

Enter organic visits and completed conversions to calculate the observed conversion rate, or use a target rate to estimate potential conversions at the same traffic level. The output also shows visits per conversion and the conversion gap. This makes the tool useful for comparing landing pages, validating campaign assumptions, and deciding whether growth should come from more traffic, better on-page conversion, or both.

SEO Conversion Rate (%) = Conversions ÷ Organic Visits × 100 Conversions at Target = Organic Visits × Target Rate ÷ 100

Conversions and visits must refer to the same period, property, and attribution setup. The calculator assumes one conversion count can be compared directly with visit count; when a user can convert multiple times, the rate may exceed a user-based rate and should be labeled accordingly.

What the result means

The conversion rate is the share of organic visits that produced the selected action. The target scenario shows potential conversions without changing traffic.

Attribution windows and cross-device behavior can change the reported conversion count even when underlying customer behavior is unchanged.

Given: 25,000 organic visits, 875 conversions, and a target conversion rate of 4.5%.

Calculation:
Conversion rate = 875 ÷ 25,000 × 100 = 3.50%
Conversions at target = 25,000 × 4.5% = 1,125
Conversion gap = 1,125 − 875 = 250

Result: The observed SEO conversion rate is 3.50%. Reaching 4.5% at the same traffic volume would add 250 conversions.

Can I use revenue transactions as conversions?

Yes. Count completed transactions and use organic visits from the same scope and period.

Why might analytics tools report different rates?

They may use different session definitions, attribution models, consent settings, or conversion windows. Compare results only after aligning those rules.

Can conversions exceed visits?

They can when multiple conversion events are allowed per visit. This calculator limits conversions to visits, so use a unique or primary conversion count.

Should I optimize traffic or conversion rate first?

The answer depends on the size and economics of each opportunity. The target scenario helps quantify the gain from rate improvement at current traffic.

Is conversion rate the same as close rate?

No. Conversion rate covers the action defined on the site; close rate usually measures the share of leads that become customers later.
