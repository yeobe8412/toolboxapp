Home Care No Show Impact Estimator

Estimate how missed or unavailable home care visits affect weekly delivered volume, field time, and potential visit value. The calculator applies a missed-visit rate to scheduled visits and combines it with the service and travel time that had been reserved for each visit.In home care, not every missed visit creates the same loss. Staff may learn about a cancellation before travel, may be able to reroute, or may still incur most of the drive time. Use the travel-loss share input to model how much of the planned travel time is typically consumed when a visit does not occur.

5. Optional value estimate
Add a per-visit value to estimate potential value not realized, or use 0 when you only need operational time impacts.

Missed visits = Scheduled visits × Missed-visit rate % ÷ 100 Delivered visits = Scheduled visits − Missed visits Undelivered service hours = Missed visits × Service minutes ÷ 60 Travel hours still incurred = Missed visits × Travel minutes × Travel-loss share % ÷ 100 ÷ 60 Potential visit value not realized = Missed visits × Average value per completed visit

What the result means

The main result is the estimated count of scheduled home visits that are not delivered. The breakdown separates undelivered service time from travel time that may still be consumed.

Actual impact depends on cancellation timing, rerouting, replacement visits, payer rules, and whether a missed visit is later rescheduled.

Given

  • 200 scheduled visits
  • 7% missed-visit rate
  • 60 service minutes
  • 30 travel minutes
  • 40% of travel still incurred
  • $90 visit value

Calculation

Missed visits = 200 × 0.07 = 14. Delivered visits = 186. Undelivered service = 14 × 60 ÷ 60 = 14 hours. Incurred travel = 14 × 30 × 0.40 ÷ 60 = 2.8 hours. Potential value = 14 × $90 = $1,260.

Result

Estimated missed visits: 14; 14.0 service hours not delivered; 2.8 travel hours still consumed.

This separates the service opportunity lost from the travel effort that may have already occurred before the missed visit was confirmed.

What should count as a missed home care visit?

Use the same operational definition used in your source data. Some agencies separate patient cancellations, refusals, unavailable-at-door events, and staff cancellations.

Why is travel loss a separate percentage?

A missed visit may be known before departure or only after staff have traveled. The percentage lets you avoid assuming that every missed visit wastes either zero or all planned travel time.

Should rescheduled visits be treated as lost value?

If the visit is completed later and you are measuring ultimate revenue, you may not want to count the full value as lost. The calculator is best read as value not realized in the originally scheduled slot.

Can missed visits ever increase capacity elsewhere?

Yes. Early notice may allow a replacement visit or a reroute. In that case the gross missed count remains useful, but net unused capacity can be much smaller.

How does this affect staffing estimates?

Persistent missed visits reduce realized workload, but staffing should not automatically be cut by the same percentage because travel, minimum coverage, schedule variability, and care obligations can remain.
